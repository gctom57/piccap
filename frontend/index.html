About

Ambient lighting for LG webOS TVs

PicCap and hyperion-webos

PicCap is the app you are looking at. It ships and controls hyperion-webos, a background service that captures the picture through the TV's own capture interfaces (found by reverse engineering), scales it down and sends it to a receiver such as Hyperion or HyperHDR, which drives your LEDs.

There is no official way to capture DRM-protected streams such as Netflix or Prime Video. HDMI inputs are not affected, so playing through a PC, Fire TV stick or Chromecast keeps your LEDs working.

PicCap needs root and sets it up on first start through the Homebrew Channel.
